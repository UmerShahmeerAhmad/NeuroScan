How access works

Every doctor is checked by a person before they can upload a single image.

  1. Register with your details

    Enter your name, institution, designation and medical license number.

  2. The administrator verifies you

    Your details are reviewed and your account is approved or declined. If it is declined, you are told why.

  3. Upload an MRI image

    Once approved, log in and drop in a JPG or PNG slice. Add a case reference if you want to find it later.

  4. Read the result

    See the predicted class, the confidence, and the probability for every class. Each scan is saved to your private history.

What a reading includes

Glioma
A tumor that arises from the brain's glial cells.
Meningioma
Usually a slow-growing tumor of the membranes around the brain.
Pituitary tumor
A growth in the pituitary gland at the base of the brain.
No tumor
No tumor pattern found in the submitted slice.

Built around trust

Doctors only

Institution, designation and license number are checked before an account can log in, and access can be withdrawn at any time.

Private by default

Uploaded images are shown only to the doctor who uploaded them. The administrator sees activity totals, never images or case references.

Honest about uncertainty

Every result shows the full probability breakdown, and low-confidence readings are flagged for manual review.

NeuroScan AI is a final year project. Its readings are decision support and do not replace a radiologist's diagnosis.
